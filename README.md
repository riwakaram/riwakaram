# Hi, I'm Riwa Karam

Ph.D. Candidate in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at UC Irvine,
researching collaborative multi-agent systems, robotics, machine learning,
optimization, and human-swarm interaction.

## Research Interests

- Collaborative multi-agent and multi-robot systems
- Machine learning for robotic behavior
- Optimization and resource allocation
- Human-swarm interaction
- Robotics testbeds and experimental systems

## Current Work

I am a member of the UCI Robot Ecology Lab, advised by Professors Magnus
Egerstedt and Yanning Shen. My research examines how heterogeneous robotic
teams can collaborate across tasks, capabilities, and environments.

## Selected Work

- **Learning Altruistic Collaboration in Heterogeneous Multi-Team Systems**
- **Collaboration in Multi-Robot Systems: Taxonomy and Survey over Frameworks for Collaboration**
- **Resource Allocation for Multi-Team Collaboration Based on Hamilton's Rule**
- **A Graphical Interface for Specifying and Establishing Multi-Robot Formations**
