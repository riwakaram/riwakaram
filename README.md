## Research

Member of the [UCI Robot Ecology Lab](https://faculty.sites.uci.edu/robotecology/), advised by Professors **Magnus Egerstedt** and **Yanning Shen**.

A central question: **when does collaboration let robotic teams achieve something they could not accomplish alone?**

- **Multi-agent collaboration:** combining complementary capabilities and sharing resources across teams with different objectives.
- **Learning and optimization:** connecting ecology-inspired models of altruism with graph neural networks to guide resource allocation.
- **Human–swarm interaction:** translating freehand drawings into robot formations through intuitive interfaces.
- **Experimental robotics:** supporting Robotarium hardware, motion capture, and reproducible multi-robot experiments.
